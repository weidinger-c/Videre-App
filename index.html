Videre

A photo & video viewer for macOS. Open one file and browse the whole folder, play videos in the same window without switching apps, or flip to a gallery of thumbnails — with JPEG, PNG, HEIC, GIF, TIFF, BMP, WebP, MP4, MOV and M4V all in one place. Beautiful in Dark Mode, with colored themes for a more personal touch.

Privacy Policy
What Videre does and does not do with your data. Short version: nothing is collected — no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and your files never leave your Mac.
Support
Questions, bug reports and feature requests — and answers to the common ones, from supported formats to themes and refunds.
